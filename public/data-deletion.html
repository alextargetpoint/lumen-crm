Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

Lumen CRM (operated by TargetPoint, Verona, Italy) respects your right to have your personal data deleted. This page explains how to request deletion of your data from Lumen.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@targetpoint.agency with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the phone number and/or name associated with your data so we can locate it.
  3. We will verify the request and delete the associated personal data (contact details, message history, call recordings and transcripts) from Lumen within 30 days, and confirm by email.
Note: If your data is managed by an agency that uses Lumen to communicate with you, that agency is the data controller. We will delete the data we hold and, where applicable, forward your request to the agency so they can act on it as well.

What gets deleted

Upon a verified request we remove your personal identifiers, conversation content, and any call recordings/transcripts tied to you, except where we are legally required to retain certain records. Backups are purged on their normal rotation cycle.

For developers / platform callback

Lumen also supports an automated data‑deletion callback for platform integrations. Programmatic deletion requests received with a valid signed request are processed automatically and a confirmation code is returned. For assistance, contact info@targetpoint.agency.
