LLumen CRM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Lumen CRM (“Lumen”, “we”, “us”) is a business software platform operated by TargetPoint (Verona, Italy) that real‑estate agencies use to communicate with and qualify their own leads across WhatsApp, phone and other channels. This policy explains what data we process and how. Lumen is a business tool used by agencies (our “Customers”) to serve their own end‑customers (“Leads”); it is not a general‑purpose consumer chatbot.

1. Who is responsible for your data

For the personal data of Leads, the agency (our Customer) is the data controller and Lumen acts as a data processor on the agency’s instructions. For account holders (agency staff/brokers using Lumen), Lumen is the controller.

2. What data we process

  • Lead contact details — name, phone number, email and similar identifiers the Lead submitted to the agency.
  • Conversation content — messages (text, images, video, files, voice notes) exchanged between the agency and the Lead over WhatsApp and other channels, needed to answer the Lead’s request.
  • Call recordings & transcripts — where a phone or WhatsApp call is recorded for quality and record‑keeping. Recording is done only with prior notice and the Lead’s consent, and recordings are stored securely (see §6).
  • WhatsApp Business data — for agencies connecting their WhatsApp Business Account via Meta’s Embedded Signup, we process business‑account and phone‑number metadata, message templates and delivery/quality status to operate messaging on the agency’s behalf.
  • Account & usage data — broker names, login and session data, and product usage needed to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

  • To let the agency receive, answer and qualify its own Leads (purpose, timeline, budget, property type) and route them to the right broker.
  • To send and receive business messages the Lead requested, within the messaging platforms’ rules (including WhatsApp’s 24‑hour customer‑service window).
  • To place and record calls (with consent) and attach transcripts to the Lead’s record for the agency’s internal follow‑up.
  • To operate, secure and improve the service.

Any AI assistance in Lumen is a business‑specific feature that helps the agency qualify and respond to its own Leads. It is not a general‑purpose assistant and is not offered as a standalone chatbot to the public.

4. WhatsApp / Meta

Messaging is delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.). Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta’s and WhatsApp’s own terms and policies. We process WhatsApp data only to provide the messaging features described above and in accordance with Meta’s Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

5. Sharing with third parties

We share data only with service providers strictly necessary to run the platform, under contract and confidentiality: Meta/WhatsApp (messaging), telephony providers (call connection & recording), and AI/transcription providers (qualification assistance and call transcription). We do not sell personal data.

6. Security & retention

Access is authenticated and role‑restricted; brokers can only see the Leads assigned to them, enforced on the server. Secrets and tokens are stored securely and never exposed to unauthorized users. Media and recordings are stored under non‑guessable identifiers. We keep personal data only as long as needed for the agency’s legitimate business or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

7. Your rights & data deletion

Leads and users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal data. See our Data Deletion page for how to request deletion, or contact us at info@targetpoint.agency. If your data is controlled by an agency using Lumen, we will act on that agency’s instruction and can forward your request to them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date reflects the latest version.
